WAYNE COUNTY PROUD SINCE 2022

We smash for
the lake life.

Cumberland Smash started with two brothers who grew up fishing these waters and wanted to bring something special to the shoreline — proper smashburgers with that perfect crispy edge, made fresh and served with a smile.

Today we roll through Monticello, the boat ramps, Memorial Park, and the best lakeside spots around the Cumberland. We show up for the early tournaments, the weekend crowds, and the families who make this place home.
